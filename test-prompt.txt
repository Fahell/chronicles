# Round 12 — Runtime validation prompt (fixes + day-cycle feats + 2-NPC scene)

You are validating the latest build of **Chronicles** (a visual-novel RPG built
on this Perchance generator). This round ships the **round-11 fixes** together
with the **day-cycle milestone** — one combined round, one report.

**Changes since round 10:**
1. **Client-side RMBG fixed** (round-11 fix, re-attempted): the ORT proxy
   worker now bootstraps its factory as a **same-origin blob** (fetch the
   factory `.mjs` from the jsdelivr CDN → `URL.createObjectURL` →
   `wasmPaths: { wasm: CDN, mjs: blobURL }`). Expect `model ready` +
   `inference done` on the platform and `cutout-cache: hit` on warm loads —
   the round-9/10 gate (`no available backend found` / `[wasm] [object Event]`)
   must be gone.
2. **Payload fixed** (round-11): the scene art prompts no longer leak into the
   NPC/narrator payloads — `WHERE YOU ARE` / `SCENE` carry only the authored
   visual descriptions (narrative-spec §2.2, "never send the recipe").
3. **Esc dual behavior fixed** (round-11): with a dialogue open, Esc closes
   the dialogue only — it must NOT also open the pause.
4. **Fog redesigned** (round-11): a low creeping fog band anchored at the
   bottom of the frame (dense at the characters' feet, fading toward the
   horizon) + a subtle veil at the horizon line — NOT a full-frame whitewash.
   The sky stays clear; characters keep contrast.
5. **NEW — In-game day clock** (day-cycle-spec §3): days with
   **Morning / Afternoon / Night** periods; each period holds up to **3
   interactions** (dialogue turns); when a period's budget is spent the clock
   advances; when Night's budget is spent the day ends. A **"Rest / end the
   day"** button in the HUD ends the day by choice. The current period is
   injected into NPC/narrator payloads as a named **TIME OF DAY** section.
6. **NEW — Relationship web** (relationships-spec §2/§5): directed bonds
   between the user and each NPC (A→B may differ from B→A), intensity
   −100..+100, with named tiers (Enemy … Intimate). Persists in IndexedDB.
7. **NEW — Per-character raw log** (day-cycle-spec §4): every dialogue turn
   and player action is appended to the NPC's character log (append-only,
   tagged by type/day/period). Visible in the dev inspector **Logs tab**.
8. **NEW — End-of-day processing run** (day-cycle-spec §5): when the day ends
   (Rest or Night exhaustion), one batched LLM call per up-to-2 NPCs scores
   the day (user→NPC and NPC→user, bounded ±5/day), stores a **day summary**
   per NPC, applies the deltas to the relationship web, and shows a discreet
   **recap** ("You grew closer to X." — no raw numbers).
9. **NEW — Second co-present NPC** (round-12 decision): the scene now has the
   player + **two** NPCs. Each NPC knows the other is present (appearance
   only — backgrounds stay private). The HUD has **two** "Talk to …" buttons.

Do the checks **in order**, driving the actual UI. The AI plugins are real
here — generations take time; the loading indicators are part of what we
validate. Report everything with **console lines verbatim** (keep the `[rpg]`
logs). Before you start, read `src/README.md` for project context and the
